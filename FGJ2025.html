Bubble Boom

Bubble Boom was developed during the Finnish Game Jam 2025, using Unity. Players progress through a handcrafted puzzle-adventure level by solving interconnected challenges that test their observation and problem-solving skills. My main responsibilities included modeling the player character, creating animations, and developing a cartoon shader for the character using Unity Shader Graph.
