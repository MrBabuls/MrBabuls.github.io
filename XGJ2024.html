Lost in the Morgue

Lost in the Morgue was created during the Xamk Game Jam 2024 using Unity. Players awaken in a mysterious morgue and must find a way to unlock a sealed room. To escape, they explore the environment, uncover clues, and solve interconnected puzzles that gradually reveal the path forward. My role in the team was as a programmer and 3D artist. I contributed to the development of the game by creating 3D assets and implementing gameplay features.
