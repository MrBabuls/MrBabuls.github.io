Born to Burn

Born to Burn is an adventure game created for the Xamk Game Jam 2025 Fall. Players explore a mysterious graveyard where they must find and relight all the candles scattered throughout the area. The environment is filled with dangers, including roaming skeletons, rain clouds that can extinguish flames, and spreading fires that threaten the player's character. My main responsibilities included modeling the player character, creating its rig and animations, producing textures, and modifying existing assets such as shaders and models.
