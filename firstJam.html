SOS - Submarine of Senseless

SOS - Submarine of Senseless was created during the Game Jam Univali + Xamk 2022, using Unity. Players work together to repair a damaged submarine while trying to survive the chaos caused by an unstable captain. The goal is to fix the submarine before time runs out. The game supports up to four players and focuses on teamwork and quick problem-solving.

My role in the team was as a programmer. I was responsible for creating the UI, implementing character movement using Unity's Input System, and adding music and sound effects to the project. I also contributed to the animation work by creating menu character movements and the wheel interaction used when the captain controls the submarine.
