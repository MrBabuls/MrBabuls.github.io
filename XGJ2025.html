Act One

Act One is an adventure horror game created for the Xamk Game Jam 2025 Spring using Unity. Players explore an eerie theater and must recover three ancient artifacts belonging to the Greek gods: Zeus’s ring, Hades’s book, and Poseidon’s bottle. As the player searches for the missing items, their sanity slowly begins to decline, blurring the line between reality and illusion. My main responsibilities included modeling the enemy character, creating its rig and animations, producing textures, and modifying existing assets and their materials.
